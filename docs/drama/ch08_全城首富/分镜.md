## 段 1 · 高定登台（10 秒）

- 画面：发布会，苏晚一身星河藏青高定从舞台中央走向观众，披风在身后展开。台下观众起立，闪光灯、举起的手机。
- 字幕：无台词。屏幕下方小字“晚星集团 · 年度发布会”。
- 这是全剧最贵的一个镜头，也是买量主素材，必须用 q4，可多抽几次。
- 音效：全场骤静后掌声爆发，史诗感的弦乐。

```
In a grand dark auditorium, the young woman in the starry midnight-blue couture gown walks slowly down the center of the stage toward the audience, her crystal-scattered cape flowing and trailing behind her, the crystals on her gown glittering under the spotlight. She raises her chin with a serene, powerful look. The audience in the foreground rises to its feet, camera flashes going off and phones held up.

Camera: slow low-angle push-in from the audience toward her, keeping her full figure in frame.

Keep her face, hairstyle and outfit exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, majestic natural motion.

Sound: a hush, then thunderous applause, a sweeping epic string score.
```

## 段 2 · 苏婉柔被曝光（8 秒）

- 画面：大屏幕亮起（剪辑时叠加当年医院调包记录的模糊画面，不让模型生成文字），记者冲向观众席里的苏婉柔，闪光灯打在她脸上。她妆哭花了，蝴蝶结歪了，捂着脸挤出人群。
- 台词：记者（嘈杂）“苏小姐，当年调包的事你知情吗？”（14 字）
- 音效：快门声连成一片，嘈杂的追问。

```
In the audience of the launch event, reporters swarm around the young woman with the big cream bow, thrusting microphones and cameras at her, flashes exploding in her face. Her makeup is smeared with tears and her bow hangs askew. She covers her face with one hand and shoves her way through the crowd trying to escape, people around her staring and pointing.

Camera: handheld-style tracking shot following her through the crowd.

Keep her face, hairstyle and outfit exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, chaotic natural motion.

Sound: a storm of camera shutters, overlapping shouted questions.
```

## 段 3 · 回望三年（10 秒）

- 画面：散场后的空舞台，苏晚一个人站在台前，背对镜头，金色彩带慢慢飘落。她微微回头，平静地笑。
- 旁白：“三年前，所有人都在笑我。现在，他们排队等我回头。可我不会回头了。”（30 字，约 7.5 秒）
- 和序章最后一句旁白首尾呼应；画面也和序章雨夜推车的背影呼应。
- 音效：空旷大厅的回声，序章那个钢琴单音再次出现，然后展开成完整旋律。

```
The auditorium after the event, empty and quiet. The young woman in the starry midnight-blue gown stands alone at the front edge of the stage, seen from behind, her cape spread around her. Golden confetti drifts slowly down through the soft stage light. After a moment she turns her head slightly over her shoulder, a quiet, peaceful smile on her face.

Camera: slow crane-like move from high behind her, gently descending and drifting closer.

Keep her face, hairstyle and outfit exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, slow graceful motion.

Sound: a soft echo in the empty hall, a single piano note that grows into a full gentle melody.
```

## 段 4 · 结局 · 陆屿（10 秒）

- 画面：舞台台阶下，陆屿等着她，向她伸出手。她走下最后一级台阶，把手放进他手里。金色彩带在两人周围飘落。
- 台词：陆屿“回家吗？”（3 字）苏晚“嗯，回家。”（4 字）——“家”指外婆的老房子，接羁绊第 5 段“屋顶看云城的灯”。
- 播完出“全剧终”和图鉴入口。
- 音效：主题曲进来。

```
At the foot of the stage steps, the young man in the navy linen shirt waits and holds out his hand with a gentle smile, asking a short quiet question. The young woman in the starry gown descends the last step, places her hand in his and answers with a soft, happy smile. He closes his fingers around hers. Golden confetti drifts down around them as they turn and walk away together hand in hand.

Camera: slow push-in on the two of them, ending on their joined hands.

Keep both faces, hairstyles and outfits exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action romance drama look, soft natural motion.

Sound: the main theme song swelling in.
```
