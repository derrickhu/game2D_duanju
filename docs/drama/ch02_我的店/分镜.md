## 段 1 · 周房东刁难（10 秒）

- 路线说明：一来一回的对白戏，用 q3-drama。主体：苏晚 B（face / front / 34）、周房东（face / front）、甜品店门口空景；首帧作构图参考。
- 画面：临街空铺面门口，周房东捧着泡茶杯，上下打量苏晚，嗤笑。苏晚攥紧手里的租房广告。
- 台词：周房东“假千金也想开店？押金翻三倍。”（13 字，约 3.5 秒）

The young woman grips the rental flyer in her hand, her jaw tightening, saying nothing.

Shots: medium two-shot; close-up of the man's smirk and the keys; close-up of her hand crumpling the flyer and her eyes turning cold.

## 段 2 · 拍钱买楼（8 秒）

- 场景：店内；人物：苏晚 B、周房东
- 画面：苏晚把一沓现金拍在大理石小桌上，俯身直视坐着的周房东。他手里的茶杯一抖，茶水洒出来。
- 台词：苏晚“这栋楼，你开个价。”（8 字，约 2 秒）
- 这是本章的打脸名场面，也是买量素材候选。
- 音效：现金拍桌的一声闷响（要重），鼓点进来。

Vidu 提示词：

```
The young woman in the navy apron slams a thick stack of banknotes down on the round marble table, leans forward with both hands on the table and stares coolly down at the seated balding man, speaking calmly. His mouth falls open, his hand jerks and green tea splashes out of his glass flask onto his paisley shirt; he looks from the money up to her face in disbelief.

Camera: quick slight push-in on the slam, then hold on a medium two-shot.

Keep both faces, hairstyles and outfits exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, natural motion.

Sound: a heavy thud of money on marble, a sharp drum hit, tea splashing.
```
