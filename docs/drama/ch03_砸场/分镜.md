## 段 1 · 苏婉柔吐甜品（8 秒）

- 主体（q3-drama）：苏晚 B（face / front）、傅景深 A（face / front）、苏婉柔（face / front）、甜品店内空景。
- 画面：两人进店，苏婉柔尝一口草莓奶油点心，当场吐进纸巾，嫌弃地把盘子推开。傅景深搂着她冷笑。柜台后的苏晚面无表情。
- 台词：苏婉柔“这种东西也敢卖？”（8 字，约 2 秒，娇声、拖长尾音）
- 音效：店内轻音乐被打断，顾客的抽气声。

```
Inside a navy-blue dessert shop. A young woman in a white lace dress with a big cream bow takes one dainty bite of a strawberry cream pastry, then spits it into a napkin with exaggerated disgust and pushes the plate away with two fingers, saying in a sweet whiny voice: "这种东西也敢卖？" The young man in the light grey suit with his arm around her smirks arrogantly. Behind the glass counter, the young woman in the navy apron watches them in silence, her face calm and cold. Customers turn to stare.

Shots: medium shot of the lace-dress woman spitting into the napkin; reaction shot of the man's smirk; close-up of the apron woman's cold calm eyes.

Cinematic live-action Chinese short drama, realistic faces, natural acting.
```

## 段 2 · “回来求我”（10 秒）

- 画面：傅景深一肘撑在柜台上，两指夹着一张黑卡递过去，居高临下。苏晚站得笔直，不接。
- 台词：傅景深“晚晚，回来求我，我还可以给你一个位置。”（17 字，约 4.5 秒）
- 表演要点：他的语气是施舍，以为她会心动；她一动不动，最后轻轻笑了一下。
- 音效：安静，只有展示柜的冷柜嗡声。

```
The young man in the light grey suit leans one elbow on the glass pastry counter and tilts toward the young woman in the navy apron with a condescending half-smile, holding out a black credit card between two fingers and speaking slowly as if granting a favor. She stands perfectly still behind the counter, looking back at him without blinking, not reaching for the card. After a beat, the corner of her mouth lifts in a faint, cold, almost pitying smile.

Camera: slow push-in on her face over his shoulder.

Keep both faces, hairstyles and outfits exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, subtle natural acting.

Sound: quiet shop, the low hum of the display fridge.
```

## 段 3 · 顾客站出来（10 秒）

- 主体（q3-drama）：傅景深 A（face / front）、苏婉柔（face / front）、苏晚 B（face）、甜品店内空景。
- 画面：排队的顾客一个接一个站出来围住两人：白领、大学生、拎菜篮的阿姨。傅景深尴尬后退，苏婉柔躲到他身后。
- 台词：顾客（阿姨，大嗓门）“我们排了两个小时，你们算老几？”（14 字，约 3.5 秒）；其他顾客附和“就是！”
- 音效：人群七嘴八舌，越来越响。

```
Inside the navy dessert shop. The queuing customers step forward one after another and surround the young man in the light grey suit and the young woman in the white lace dress: an office worker with a lanyard, two college girls, and a loud middle-aged auntie holding a vegetable basket, who points at the man and shouts: "我们排了两个小时，你们算老几？" The others nod and shout in agreement. The man steps back awkwardly, his arrogance cracking; the lace-dress woman hides behind his arm with teary frightened eyes. Behind the counter, the young woman in the navy apron watches with a faint smile.

Shots: wide shot of the crowd closing in; medium shot of the auntie shouting; reaction two-shot of the couple backing away.

Cinematic live-action Chinese short drama, realistic faces, natural lively acting.
```

## 段 4 · 十倍账单（8 秒）

- 画面：苏晚两指把一张长长的账单推到傅景深面前，礼貌地笑。他低头看账单，笑容僵住。
- 台词：苏晚“插队，按十倍收费。慢走不送。”（12 字，约 3 秒）
- 音效：纸滑过玻璃的沙沙声，一声鼓点，顾客的笑声和掌声。

```
The young woman in the navy apron slides a long paper receipt across the glass counter toward the young man with two fingers, smiling politely, her eyes sharp, and speaks two short calm sentences. He looks down at the receipt; his arrogant smile freezes and slowly cracks into stunned disbelief. Customers in the background burst into laughter and applause.

Camera: medium close-up over his shoulder on her face, then a quick cut-feel push-in to his frozen face in profile.

Keep both faces, hairstyles and outfits exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, natural motion.

Sound: paper sliding on glass, a sharp drum hit, laughter and applause.
```
