## 段 1 · 下战书（10 秒）

- 主体（q3-drama）：霍霆琛 A（face / front）、苏晚 D（face / front）、晚星顶楼空景。
- 画面：霍霆琛站在她办公桌前，把一个黑色文件夹放到桌上，单手插兜。苏晚靠在椅背上跷着腿，迎着他的目光。
- 台词：霍霆琛“苏小姐，一个月，我就能挤垮你。”（13 字）苏晚“霍总可以试试。”（6 字）
- 这是霍霆琛羁绊线的初遇（策划案原写“酒会上”，这里改成上门下战书，更贴主线），结束后解锁“霍霆琛”。
- 音效：落地窗外的风声，文件夹落桌的一声。

```
A penthouse office with glass walls over the city at golden hour. A tall man in a deep jade-green three-piece suit stands in front of the desk, one hand in his pocket, and drops a black folder onto the desk. He looks down at the young woman in the white suit with a cold arrogant half-smile: "苏小姐，一个月，我就能挤垮你。" She leans back in her chair with her legs crossed, meets his eyes without blinking, and answers lightly: "霍总可以试试。" His eyebrow lifts slightly.

Shots: medium-wide two-shot; close-up of the folder landing; close-up of him; close-up of her calm answer; close-up of his eyebrow lifting.

Cinematic live-action Chinese short drama, realistic faces, sharp restrained acting.
```

## 段 2 · 外婆的信（10 秒）

- 画面：外婆老房子，苏晚跪在樟木箱前，一手泛黄的信，一手铜色纹章。读着读着眼泪掉下来。
- 旁白（外婆的声音，苍老温柔）：“晚晚，你不是谁家多出来的孩子。你是沈家的小星星。”（23 字，约 6 秒）
- 音效：老房子的木头吱呀声，窗外的蝉鸣，温柔的钢琴。

```
In an old house filled with warm afternoon sunlight and floating dust, the young woman kneels beside an open camphor-wood chest, reading a yellowed handwritten letter. Her other hand holds a round antique brass medallion engraved with a crescent moon and a star. As she reads, tears well up and fall onto the letter; she presses the medallion to her chest and closes her eyes.

Camera: slow push-in from a medium shot to a close-up of her face and the medallion.

Keep her face, hairstyle and outfit exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, soft natural motion.

Sound: creaking old wooden floor, cicadas outside, a gentle piano.
```

## 段 3 · “我找了你很多年”（12 秒）

- 画面：老房子里，苏晚举起纹章，陆屿抬起左腕，表盘上是一模一样的纹章。她震惊地看他，他看着她，眼里全是心疼。
- 台词：苏晚“你到底是谁？”（5 字）陆屿“你是沈家唯一的外孙女。我找了你很多年。”（17 字，约 4.5 秒）
- 这一段同时是陆屿羁绊第 4 段“告白”的主线版本。
- 音效：安静，只有手表的滴答声被放大。

```
In the old house at sunset, the young woman holds up the antique brass medallion beside the young man's raised left wrist; the crest on the black dial of his antique watch is identical to the medallion. She looks up at him, stunned, tears in her eyes, and asks a short question. He looks back at her with deep, aching tenderness and answers slowly and quietly, then gently closes his hand around hers and the medallion.

Camera: close-up on the medallion and the watch side by side, then a slow rise and push-in to the two faces in profile.

Keep both faces, hairstyles and outfits exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action romance drama look, subtle natural acting.

Sound: a quiet room, the ticking of the watch amplified, a soft swelling string note.
```

## 段 4 · 霍霆琛第一次笑（8 秒）

- 画面：夜里，霍霆琛独自坐在办公桌边沿，端着威士忌看墙上的新闻：苏晚在发布会上。他冷硬的脸上第一次露出真心的笑。
- 新闻画外音：“沈氏家族律师今日宣读遗嘱，沈氏全部资产由苏晚女士继承。”（字幕，不做口型）
- 音效：新闻播报声，冰块碰杯。

```
Night in a dark minimalist office high above the city. The man in the jade-green suit sits on the edge of his desk holding a glass of whiskey, watching a news broadcast on the large wall screen showing the young woman in the white suit at a press conference. He watches for a long moment; then, for the first time, a genuine amused smile breaks across his cold face, and he lifts his glass slightly toward the screen.

Camera: slow push-in on his face lit by the screen.

Keep his face, hairstyle and outfit exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, subtle natural acting.

Sound: a muffled news broadcast, ice clinking in the glass.
```
