- 主体（q3-drama）：苏晚 D（face / front）、苏父（face）、苏母（face）、晚星会议室空景。

```

The middle-aged couple sitting opposite stare at it with pale, defeated faces. The woman in the plum dress forces a tearful smile and pleads: "晚晚，我们好歹养了你二十年……" The young woman lifts her eyes and answers calmly: "签了它，苏家还能留个名字。" The man in the pinstripe suit slowly picks up the pen with a trembling hand.

Shots: wide shot of the long table; close-up of the mother pleading; close-up of the young woman's calm answer; insert of the trembling hand lifting the pen.
```
