## 段 1 · 大屏广告（8 秒）

- 场景：闹市街头（无空景，首帧文字描述）；人物：苏晚 C、苏婉柔（广告里）
- 画面：黄昏街头，大楼外墙的巨幅屏幕上，苏婉柔甜笑着捧着新品甜品盒。苏晚停在人流里，怀里抱着外婆的手写方子，抬头看，震惊变成冷怒。
- 旁白：“那是外婆的方子。一个字都没改。”（13 字，约 3.5 秒）
- 音效：街头车流，广告甜腻的配乐，突然变闷。

```
Dusk on a busy downtown street. Pedestrians stream past. The young woman in the cream tweed jacket stands still among them, clutching an old indigo cloth-bound recipe notebook to her chest, staring up at the giant glowing billboard where a sweet-faced girl smiles and holds up a pastry box. Her grip on the notebook tightens, her eyes go from shock to cold anger.

Camera: slow low-angle push-in from behind her shoulder toward her face, the billboard looming above.

Keep her face, hairstyle and outfit exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, natural motion, city lights and traffic bokeh.

Sound: city traffic, a sugary advertisement jingle that fades to a muffled hum.
```

## 段 2 · “胜算三成”（10 秒）

- 主体（q3-drama）：苏晚 C（face / front）、沈砚 A（face / front）、律所空景。
- 画面：沈砚坐在大办公桌后翻案卷，头也不抬。苏晚站在桌前。他终于抬眼。
- 台词：沈砚“胜算三成。”（4 字）苏晚“那就三成。”（4 字）。沈砚停笔，第一次正眼看她。
- 这是沈砚羁绊线的初遇，结束后解锁“沈砚”。
- 音效：翻纸声、钢笔划纸声，一句对白后的停顿。

```
A law office with tall bookshelves. A man with gold-rimmed glasses and slicked-back hair sits behind a large dark wooden desk, reading a thick case file without looking up, and says flatly: "胜算三成。" The young woman in the cream tweed jacket standing across the desk answers immediately, calm and firm: "那就三成。" His pen stops. He slowly lifts his eyes and looks at her properly for the first time, a flicker of interest behind his glasses.

Shots: medium shot of him reading; close-up of her steady answer; close-up of his pen stopping and his eyes lifting.

Cinematic live-action Chinese short drama, realistic faces, restrained natural acting.
```

## 段 3 · 笔迹鉴定（10 秒）

- 画面：法庭上，沈砚举起笔迹鉴定报告，两页泛黄的手写方子并排。对方律师冒汗，松领口。苏晚坐在他身后。
- 台词：沈砚“这份配方的手稿，比苏氏食品成立早了二十年。”（20 字，约 5 秒）
- 音效：法庭里的低声议论突然安静，法槌一声。

```
In the courtroom, the man with gold-rimmed glasses stands at the counsel table and holds up a handwriting expert report showing two yellowed handwritten pages side by side, speaking calmly and decisively. The balding opposing lawyer across the room freezes, sweat on his forehead, and tugs at his collar. Behind the man, the young woman in the cream tweed jacket sits watching him, her eyes shining.

Camera: slow low-angle push-in on the man with the glasses as he speaks, the opposing lawyer soft in the background.

Keep all faces, hairstyles and outfits exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, subtle natural acting.

Sound: murmurs in the courtroom fading to silence, a single gavel strike at the end.
```

## 段 4 · 判决（8 秒）

- 画面：旁听席，判决宣读后，苏婉柔脸色惨白，双手在膝上攥成拳。苏母冷着脸，苏父低头。

Beside her, the woman in the plum dress stares ahead coldly and the man in the pinstripe suit lowers his head. Spectators around them whisper and glance over.

Camera: slow push-in on the bow-wearing woman's face, ending on her clenched fists.

Keep all faces, hairstyles and outfits exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, subtle natural acting.
