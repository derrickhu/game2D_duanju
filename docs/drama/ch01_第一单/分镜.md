A girl at the front takes a bite, her eyes light up, and she excitedly waves her friends over. More young people walk in from the background and join the line, which grows longer down the wet street.

Camera: slow sideways tracking shot along the growing queue, ending on the young woman at the cart.

Keep her face, hairstyle and outfit exactly as in the first frame. Cinematic live-action drama look, natural motion, wet reflective street under warm string lights.
